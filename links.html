How to get to Baan Lookmai

We are in Room C107 on the 1st floor of The Old Siam Plaza, Bangkok.

  • By MRT

    Take the MRT Blue Line to Sam Yot station. The plaza is a short walk from the station.

  • By car

    The plaza has its own car park, with the entrance behind the Chalermkrung Royal Theatre. Traffic around the area can be heavy, so allow extra time.

  • By taxi or ride-hailing app

    Set The Old Siam Plaza as your destination, then go to the 1st floor and look for Room C107.
